# Catalyst — Cross-Session Decisions

## 2026-05-30 — Strategic pivot: institutional transformation over financial restructuring
**Decision:** Brand and site lead with institutional transformation / governance / PMO / performance excellence + digital delivery layer. Financial restructuring is removed from the foreground until verifiable finance-capable partners are confirmed.
**Why:** Mohamed Abdelwahab (one of the partners) is documented in HR transformation, EFQM/SKGEP/ADAEP government excellence, PMO, GInI. No CFA, no finance track. Brief's finance pillars are unsupported by the verifiable roster. Leading with finance fails the first bank/government scrutiny.
**Evidence anchor:** A&M Q2 2023 Middle East Turnaround & Restructuring Survey — 73% advocate operational+financial restructuring together; <25% see it delivered. This is the white space and it maps to verifiable strengths.
**Reversal condition:** Recruit a credentialed restructuring or CFO-grade partner with referenceable track record.

## 2026-05-30 — Family-business beachhead is the primary wedge
**Decision:** Family business is the primary entry segment. "Legacy & Institution" suite is the packaged offer.
**Why:** ~60% UAE GDP, ~US$1tn generational wealth transfer, only 33% have robust succession plan, next-gen wants digitally-fluent + values-aligned advisors. Maps precisely to verifiable capability.
**Application:** Site IA, content engine, lead scoring, referral partnerships (private banks, family-law firms, Dubai Centre for Family Businesses) all weight toward this segment first.

## 2026-05-30 — Egypt delivery hub stays backstage
**Decision:** UAE = client-facing premium billing. Egypt = governed delivery engine. Egypt never appears in brand, site, or marketing.
**Why:** Margin protection + Arabic-fluent capacity, but market perception risk if surfaced. PDPL/Egypt DPL cross-border access must be controlled.

## 2026-05-30 — Ownership-by-architecture rule for the website
**Decision:** Every vendor is replaceable. Content in self-hostable CMS, code in own repo, leads in CRM **plus** an owned raw lead log so CRM itself is swappable. Every third-party integration sits behind an internal adapter.
**Why:** Firm sells governance; site must visibly practice it. Also the founders' explicit constraint: "you own everything."

## 2026-05-30 — Bilingual = first-class, not translation layer
**Decision:** Arabic gets per-locale slugs, metadata, fonts, fallback policy, RTL via CSS logical properties, Arabic-aware site search. Half-built Arabic = credibility killer with the target segments.
**Why:** Government, banking, and family-business buyers in UAE are Arabic-first. Most agencies fake this and it shows.
